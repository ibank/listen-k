# Code of Conduct

Listen K follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version is below; the linked document is the authoritative text.

## Our pledge

We want everyone who participates in the Listen K project — issue reporters,
translators, code contributors, and maintainers — to have a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Use clear, inclusive language. Multiple native languages are represented in
  this project; write with that in mind.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Any conduct prohibited by the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#unacceptable-behavior)
is unacceptable here. In short: no harassment, no personal attacks, and no
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — the GitHub repository,
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and any
project-adjacent communication.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, email **hello@listenk.com** with:

- What happened, with links or screenshots if available
- When and where it happened
- Anyone else involved
- Whether you would like to remain anonymous in any follow-up

Reports are handled by the maintainer. You will receive an acknowledgement
within 72 hours. We will not share your report beyond what is necessary to
act on it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take any action they consider appropriate, including
warnings, edits or deletion of contributions, or a ban from project spaces.
Enforcement decisions are final at the maintainer's discretion.
