Usage

1 Put the cursor where you want text.
2 ⇧⇧ once — recording starts (HUD appears).
3 When done speaking, ⇧⇧ again or click ✓ on the HUD.
4 The cleaned/translated text is auto-pasted into the focused field.
Cancel via ✕ on the HUD · alternate hotkey ⌘⇧Space
